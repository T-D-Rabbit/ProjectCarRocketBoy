RocketBoy – 3D model and hard-coded route (open-loop simulation)

Drag = orbit, wheel / pinch = zoom. Model: 300 mm chassis (STEP), L-clamps, SG-3851218000-60K motors, 110 mm wheels, 155 x 208 mm PCB on 40 mm standoffs with 9 TB6612FNG modules, ESP32 socket, screw terminals, expansion header; battery, buck module and caster underneath. Motion = the simulation in simulation/run_all.py (S5 route, 1 driver group enabled). Orange line = path, floor grid = 0.5 m.
