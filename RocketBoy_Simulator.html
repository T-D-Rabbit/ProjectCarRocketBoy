RocketBoy simulator 3D model + electro-mechanical model of the 9×TB6612FNG driver bank, 3S battery, SG-3851218000-60K motors and 110 mm wheels

Drive

Keys: WASD or arrows = drive/steer, Space = cut power, R = reset, P = pause, 123 = switch SW1-3, C = camera.

Settings

STBY switches
Each switch enables 3 of the 9 TB6612 modules (3 per motor channel, 1.2 A continuous each). Try: jam the wheels with only SW1 on → modules go red.
